================================================================================
DIAGNOSTIC CRITERIA USED
================================================================================

1. Coefficient of Variation (CV = std/mean):
   - CV < 0.15: Suggests truncated normal (additive process)
   - CV > 0.25: Suggests log-normal (multiplicative process)

2. Skewness:
   - |skewness| < 0.5: Roughly symmetric
   - skewness > 0.5: Right-skewed (log-normal candidate)
   - Log-transform should reduce skewness for log-normal data

3. Physical Constraints:
   - All parameters must be positive → bounded distributions needed
   - Physiological ranges known → truncation required

MODEL EQUATIONS (BERGMANN MINIMAL MODEL VARIANT)
================================================================================

1. Glucose dynamics:
   dG/dt = -p1·(G - Gb) - X·G + meal_absorption

   dI/dt = (1000·infusion/Vg) - k_clear·(I - Ib)
